ส่วนที่ I — เครื่องมือพื้นฐาน (บท 1–3)

% Change และ Asymmetry

% Change
$$ \%\Delta = \frac{\text{ใหม่} - \text{เก่า}}{\text{เก่า}} \times 100 $$
Recovery needed after −x%
$$ r = \frac{x}{100-x} \times 100\% $$
e.g. ลด 50% → ต้องขึ้น 100%

Options Payoff

$$ \text{Long Call} = \max(0, S-K) - C $$
$$ \text{Long Put} = \max(0, K-S) - P $$
Put-Call Parity
$$ C + Ke^{-rT} = P + S $$

Logarithm และ e

$$ \ln(ab) = \ln a + \ln b \qquad \ln(a^n) = n\ln a $$
Log Return
$$ r = \ln\!\left(\frac{S_1}{S_0}\right) = \ln S_1 - \ln S_0 $$
บวกข้ามเวลาได้, สมมาตร, เป็น Normal ได้

Present Value / Future Value

$$ PV = FV \cdot e^{-rT} \qquad FV = PV \cdot e^{rT} $$
PV ใน Put-Call Parity
$$ PV(K) = K e^{-rT} $$

ส่วนที่ II — สถิติและความน่าจะเป็น (บท 4–6)

Variance และ Volatility

$$ \sigma^2 = \frac{\sum(x_i-\mu)^2}{n} \qquad \sigma = \sqrt{\sigma^2} $$
Annualized Vol
$$ \sigma_{\text{annual}} = \sigma_{\text{daily}} \times \sqrt{252} $$
Covariance / Correlation
$$ \text{Cov}(X,Y) = \frac{\sum(x_i-\mu_x)(y_i-\mu_y)}{n} $$
$$ \rho = \frac{\text{Cov}(X,Y)}{\sigma_x \sigma_y} \in [-1,+1] $$

Expected Value

$$ E[X] = \sum_i x_i P(x_i) $$
$$ \text{Var}(X) = E[X^2] - (E[X])^2 $$

Probability

$$ P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cap B) $$
Conditional / Bayes
$$ P(A|B) = \frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(B)} = \frac{P(B|A)P(A)}{P(B)} $$
Combinations
$$ C(n,k) = \binom{n}{k} = \frac{n!}{k!\,(n-k)!} $$

Distributions

Binomial
$$ P(X=k) = \binom{n}{k}p^k(1-p)^{n-k} $$
Normal PDF
$$ f(x) = \frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}} $$
Z-Score / CDF
$$ Z = \frac{x-\mu}{\sigma} \qquad N(d) = P(Z \le d) $$
Lognormal
$$ \ln(S) \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu,\sigma^2) \Rightarrow S \sim \text{Lognormal} $$

ส่วนที่ III — แคลคูลัส (บท 7)

Derivatives

$$ \frac{d}{dx}(x^n) = nx^{n-1} \qquad \frac{d}{dx}(e^{ax}) = ae^{ax} $$
$$ \frac{d}{dx}[\ln(x)] = \frac{1}{x} \qquad \frac{d}{dx}[f\cdot g] = f'g + fg' $$

Greeks (Partial Derivatives of C)

Integration

$$ \int x^n dx = \frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1}+C \qquad \int e^x dx = e^x + C $$
Option price as integral
$$ C = e^{-rT}\int_0^\infty \max(0,S-K)\cdot f(S)\,dS $$

Taylor Expansion (P&L Attribution)

$$ \Delta V \approx \Delta\cdot\Delta S + \tfrac{1}{2}\Gamma(\Delta S)^2 + \Theta\cdot\Delta t + \nu\cdot\Delta\sigma $$
Directional + Convexity + Time Decay + Vol P&L

ส่วนที่ IV — Linear Algebra + Regression (บท 8–9)

Portfolio Variance

$$ \sigma^2_p = \mathbf{w}^\top \boldsymbol{\Sigma}\, \mathbf{w} $$
w = weight vector, Σ = covariance matrix
2-Asset
$$ \sigma^2_p = w_1^2\sigma_1^2 + w_2^2\sigma_2^2 + 2w_1 w_2\text{Cov}_{12} $$
Matrix Inverse (2×2)
$$ A^{-1} = \frac{1}{ad-bc}\begin{pmatrix}d & -b \\ -c & a\end{pmatrix} $$

OLS Regression

$$ \beta = \frac{\text{Cov}(X,Y)}{\text{Var}(X)} = \frac{\sum(x_i-\bar{x})(y_i-\bar{y})}{\sum(x_i-\bar{x})^2} $$
Matrix OLS
$$ \hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}} = (X^\top X)^{-1}X^\top \mathbf{y} $$
t-statistic
$$ t = \frac{\hat{\beta}}{\text{se}(\hat{\beta})} \qquad |t|>2 \Rightarrow \text{significant} $$
R² (Goodness of Fit)
$$ R^2 = 1 - \frac{\text{SS}_\text{res}}{\text{SS}_\text{tot}} \in [0,1] $$

ส่วนที่ V — Optimization (บท 10–11)

Linear Programming

$$ \max\; \mathbf{c}^\top\mathbf{x} \;\text{ s.t. } A\mathbf{x} \le \mathbf{b},\; \mathbf{x} \ge 0 $$
Optimal always at a corner point of feasible region

Lagrange Multipliers

$$ \mathcal{L}(x,\lambda) = f(x) - \lambda\,g(x) $$
Markowitz (equality form)
$$ \min\; \mathbf{w}^\top\Sigma\mathbf{w} \;\text{ s.t. }\;\mathbf{1}^\top\mathbf{w}=1,\;\boldsymbol{\mu}^\top\mathbf{w}=r^* $$

Gradient Descent

$$ \mathbf{x}_{n+1} = \mathbf{x}_n - \alpha\,\nabla f(\mathbf{x}_n) $$
α = learning rate, converges for convex f

KKT Conditions

\(1.\;\) Stationarity: \(\nabla f + \sum\mu_i\nabla g_i = 0\)
\(2.\;\) Primal feasibility: \(g_i(\mathbf{x}) \le 0\)
\(3.\;\) Dual feasibility: \(\mu_i \ge 0\)
\(4.\;\) Complementary slackness: \(\mu_i\cdot g_i = 0\)

ส่วนที่ VI — Time Series + Stochastic Calculus (บท 12–14)

Volatility Models

EWMA
$$ \sigma^2_t = \lambda\sigma^2_{t-1} + (1-\lambda)r^2_{t-1} $$
GARCH(1,1)
$$ \sigma^2_t = \omega + \alpha r^2_{t-1} + \beta\sigma^2_{t-1} $$
α+β < 1 → mean reversion; long-run σ = √(ω/(1−α−β))
Newton-Raphson IV Solver
$$ \sigma_{n+1} = \sigma_n - \frac{C_\text{BS}(\sigma_n) - C_\text{mkt}}{\mathcal{V}(\sigma_n)} $$
Vega = ∂C/∂σ; converge in 3–5 iterations

Stochastic Processes

GBM (Geometric Brownian Motion)
$$ dS = \mu S\,dt + \sigma S\,dW_t $$
Ito's Lemma
$$ df = \frac{\partial f}{\partial t}dt + \frac{\partial f}{\partial S}dS + \frac{1}{2}\frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial S^2}(dS)^2 $$
(dW)² = dt (Ito correction)
Black-Scholes PDE
$$ \frac{\partial V}{\partial t} + \frac{1}{2}\sigma^2 S^2\frac{\partial^2 V}{\partial S^2} + rS\frac{\partial V}{\partial S} - rV = 0 $$
Theta + Gamma P&L + Delta P&L = risk-free return

ส่วนที่ VII — Black-Scholes + Monte Carlo (บท 15–16)

Black-Scholes Call / Put

$$ C = S_0 N(d_1) - Ke^{-rT}N(d_2) $$
$$ P = Ke^{-rT}N(-d_2) - S_0 N(-d_1) $$
$$ d_1 = \frac{\ln(S_0/K)+(r+\sigma^2/2)T}{\sigma\sqrt{T}} \quad d_2 = d_1 - \sigma\sqrt{T} $$
BS with Dividends (Merton)
$$ C = Se^{-qT}N(d_1) - Ke^{-rT}N(d_2) $$
replace S with Se⁻ᵠᵀ; d₁ uses (r−q+σ²/2)

Monte Carlo Pricing

GBM discrete simulation
$$ S_{t+\Delta t} = S_t \exp\!\left[(r-\tfrac{\sigma^2}{2})\Delta t + \sigma\sqrt{\Delta t}\,Z\right] $$
Z ~ N(0,1) i.i.d.
MC option price
$$ \hat{C} = e^{-rT}\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^N \max(0, S_T^{(i)} - K) $$
Standard Error
$$ \text{SE} = \frac{\hat{\sigma}_\text{payoff}}{\sqrt{N}} $$
4× paths → 2× precision (SE ∝ 1/√N)
Control Variates
$$ Y^* = Y_\text{Asian} - \hat{\beta}\,(Y_\text{Vanilla,MC} - Y_\text{Vanilla,BS}) $$
