A — สูตรหลัก (One-Page Cheat Sheet)

Grid Mechanics

กำไรต่อรอบ = Q × (P_sell − P_buy) − Fees Capital ถึง Floor = Σ(i=1 to N) Q_i × P_i Soft Martingale = Q_i = Q_0 × r^(i-1) , r ∈ [1.1, 1.5] รอบต่อวัน (ประมาณ) = ATR_daily / Step Grid step (ATR) = k × ATR_t , k ∈ [0.2, 1.0] Bell curve Q_i ∝ exp(−(P_i − μ)² / 2σ²)

Kelly Sizing

Kelly f* = (p × b − q) / b Fractional Kelly = 0.25 × f* (conservative) Capital deployed = f* × total_capital p = win probability, b = win/loss ratio, q = 1 − p

Hurst Exponent

log(R/S) = H × log(N) + C H < 0.5: mean-reverting (Grid ดี) H = 0.5: random walk H > 0.5: trending (Grid หยุด)

Risk Management

Drawdown DD_t = (P_peak − P_t) / P_peak Max Notional ≤ 5% capital per grid level Stop condition DD > max_dd_threshold (e.g. 8% — ดู Part 5 §5.5)
